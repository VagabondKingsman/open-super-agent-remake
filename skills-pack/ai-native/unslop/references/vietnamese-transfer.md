# Transfer from Vietnamese (T1-T12)

Patterns that appear when a Vietnamese speaker writes English, or when a model translates a Vietnamese brief phrase by phrase. Each row gives the Vietnamese source, the English it tends to produce, and the fix.

When writing from a Vietnamese brief, translate the meaning, not the sentences. Read the whole brief, decide what the post says, then write it in English from scratch. Most T errors come from keeping the Vietnamese sentence shape.

## T1. Calqued openers and slogans

| Vietnamese | Calque | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Trong thời đại công nghệ 4.0 | In the era of technology 4.0 | delete; start with the point |
| Trong thời đại số | In the digital age | delete |
| Ngày nay | Nowadays, | delete, or give the date or trend with a number |
| Như chúng ta đã biết | As we all know | delete |
| Trong bối cảnh hội nhập | In the context of integration / globalization | delete, or name the actual change |
| Không thể phủ nhận rằng | It cannot be denied that | delete; state the claim |
| Đóng vai trò quan trọng | plays an important role | say what it does |

## T5. Articles

Vietnamese has no articles, so this is the most frequent error. Rules that cover most cases:

- **First mention, singular countable:** a/an. "We built a dashboard."
- **Second mention or known to the reader:** the. "The dashboard shows…"
- **Unique or specific:** the. "The best option", "the first step", "the API we use".
- **General plural or uncountable:** no article. "Customers want speed." "Software changes fast."
- **Proper names:** usually none (Google, Vietnam), but "the United States", "the Mekong".
- **Jobs and roles:** "She is a designer", not "She is designer".
- **Common misses:** "It is good idea" → "It is a good idea". "We use the social media to…" → "We use social media to…". "In the 2025" → "In 2025".

## T6. Plural and countability

Uncountable in English (no -s, no "a"): information, advice, feedback, equipment, research, software, knowledge, homework, furniture, evidence, progress, news, traffic, staff (as a group). Write "two pieces of advice", "a lot of feedback".

Agreement: "The list of features are long" → "The list of features is long". "Each of the plans include" → "Each of the plans includes".

## T7. Tense drift

Vietnamese marks time with words (đã, đang, sẽ) or not at all. English marks it on the verb.

- How something works, general truths, tutorials: present simple. "The app syncs every hour."
- A finished event: past simple. "We launched in March."
- A result that still matters, with no fixed past time: present perfect. "We have cut load time by half."
- Plans: will or present continuous. "We're adding exports next month."

Don't switch tense mid-paragraph without a reason. "Last year we launch the app and it grows fast" → "Last year we launched the app, and it grew fast."

## T9. Comma splices and run-ons

Vietnamese chains clauses with commas: "Sản phẩm rẻ, dễ dùng, phù hợp người mới."

Calque: "The product is cheap, it is easy to use, it suits beginners."
Fix: "The product is cheap and easy to use, so it suits beginners." Or split into sentences.

Test: if both sides of a comma could stand alone as sentences, a comma is not enough.

## T10. Topic-comment and dropped subjects

| Calque | Fix |
|---|---|
| This product, it is very good. | This product is good at X. |
| Is very useful for beginners. | It's useful for beginners. |
| About price, we have three plans. | We have three price plans. |
| For me, I think that… | I think… |

## T11. Too formal for a blog

| Calque | Fix |
|---|---|

| We would like to inform you that | delete; say it |
| Hereby / herein / aforementioned | delete or plain words |
| We are honored to introduce | Meet X / Here's X |
| Please kindly | Please |
| Thank you for reading this article. | delete |
